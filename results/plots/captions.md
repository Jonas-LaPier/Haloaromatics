# Correlation figures (font: Cambria)

## correlations_b3lyp_gas.png

Figure 1. Natural logarithm of the observed pseudo-first-order rate constants (k_obs, h⁻¹) for electrochemical dehalogenation at −2.0 V vs SHE (uncompensated potential, without iR-drop compensation) [add electrode, cell, electrolyte and pH], plotted against descriptors computed at B3LYP/6-311++G(d), gas phase. Filled gray circles are published bromobenzene data, open circles are new chlorobenzene data and gray squares are published PBDE data. Error bars are ±1 standard error of k_obs propagated to ln k_obs (no standard errors are available for the chlorobenzenes). The dotted line is the least-squares fit to the bromobenzenes ((a) ΔG Ar• + e⁻ → Ar⁻: R² = 0.95, n = 7; (b) ΔG ArX•⁻ + e⁻ → Ar⁻ + X⁻: R² = 0.92, n = 7; (c) ΔG ArX•⁻ → Ar• + X⁻: R² = 0.92, n = 7; (d) LUMO of the radical anion ArX•⁻: R² = 0.92, n = 7; (e) ΔG ArX + 2e⁻ → Ar⁻ + X⁻: R² = 0.91, n = 7; (f) number of halogens: R² = 0.86, n = 7). The other compounds are predicted by that fit.
